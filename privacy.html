Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: this website does not use cookies, analytics, adverts or third-party scripts, and it does not collect personal data. Our apps have no accounts or tracking and keep your progress on your own device.

This website

madebyfavor.com is a static website. It has no sign-up, no forms, no cookies, no analytics and no embedded content from other companies. It loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties.

Display preferences

If you change the colour mode or theme, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (under the keys mbf-mode and mbf-theme) so the site looks the same next time. These values stay on your device and are never sent to us. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not receive or use these logs. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Email

If you email support@madebyfavor.com, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and to deal with your enquiry. We do not add you to a mailing list or share your details with anyone else.

Our apps

Langto, Playto, DevPREP, Verse Recall and Sortile are designed to work without accounts, adverts or tracking. Your progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Where an app offers a feature that needs a device permission, such as the microphone for speaking practice or tuning, your browser asks you first and you can decline. Each app's own privacy information applies to that app:

  • Langto
  • Playto
  • DevPREP
  • Verse Recall
  • Sortile privacy policy

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date below. Questions about privacy are welcome at support@madebyfavor.com.
