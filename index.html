Services

What we can help with

Bring us in for a focused piece of work or for the whole journey. We work in small, visible steps so you always know what is done and what is next.

  • Product strategy

    Clarify the problem, the people it is for and the smallest version worth building. You get a clear scope, a clickable prototype and a realistic plan.

  • Web & PWA apps

    Responsive, installable web apps that load quickly, work offline where it matters and keep data on the device unless there is a reason not to.

  • Cloud & delivery

    Simple, low-cost hosting, automated builds and tests, and release pipelines on GitHub Actions and Azure — so shipping is routine, not risky.

  • AI-enabled features

    Practical uses of language models, text-to-speech and speech input — designed with clear limits, sensible costs and a fallback when the model is wrong.

  • Accessibility

    Audits and fixes against WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard support, screen-reader semantics, colour contrast and reduced motion — with automated checks so it stays fixed.

  • Modernisation

    Take an ageing site or app to a modern, maintainable stack in stages — improving speed, security and ease of change without a risky big-bang rewrite.

How we work

Small steps, shipped often

Every engagement follows the same simple rhythm. You see working software early and can change direction at any point.

  1. Discover

    A short conversation about your goals, users and constraints. We agree what success looks like and what is out of scope.

  2. Shape

    We turn the idea into a scoped plan and a prototype you can click through, so decisions are made on something real.

  3. Build

    Working software in short increments, with automated tests and a live preview you can try after every change.

  4. Launch & hand over

    We ship to production, document how it all fits together and hand over everything — or stay on to keep improving it.

About

We build what we recommend

Made by Favor is an independent software consultancy. We help organisations plan and build web software, and we design, build and run our own apps — for language learning, music practice, interview preparation, scripture memorisation and puzzles.

Running real products end to end keeps our skills practical. The habits we use on our own apps are the ones we bring to yours.

  • Plain, proven technologyWe choose tools your team can maintain, not whatever is newest.
  • Accessible and fast by defaultKeyboard support, good contrast and quick load times are part of the build, not an extra.
  • Privacy firstCollect only what is needed. Our own apps have no accounts, adverts or tracking.
  • Honest about trade-offsYou hear about risks, costs and simpler options early — including when not to build something.
